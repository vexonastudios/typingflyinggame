04 FREQUENTLY ASKED

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Is Family Paintball Showdown appropriate for kids?+

Yes. It is designed as colorful, family-friendly competition. Players tag one another with paint instead of realistic violence, and private matches keep families in control of who joins.

Can we play together on our home network?+

Yes. One Windows PC can host a private LAN game and other computers on the same network can join. Trusted friends can also join an invite-only private internet match when the host enables it.

Can we play with only one or two people?+

Absolutely. Add server-controlled bots for practice, mix bots into a regular match, or let the family team battle an army of up to 30 coordinated opponents.

What computers will the game support?+

The planned public release is for Windows PCs. The game includes adjustable graphics settings intended to help families play across a mix of household computers.

Where is the download?+

It is not public yet. This page will be updated with the official release information when Family Paintball Showdown is ready.
